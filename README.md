# Pricing & Revenue Growth Analytics

## Project Overview

Analysis of retail pricing, discounts, and promotions using SQL and transactional data from the Dunnhumby Breakfast at the Frat dataset.

## Business Objective

Evaluate how pricing and promotional strategies relate to sales volume and revenue to identify opportunities for commercial optimization.

## Tools & Technologies

- MySQL Workbench
- SQL
- Dunnhumby retail dataset
- Power BI
- DAX

## Key Findings

- Feature + Display: 126.10 average units and 270.98 average sales per record.
- Feature only: 91.88 average units and 198.22 average sales per record.
- Display only: 71.98 average units and 165.34 average sales per record.
- Temporary Price Reduction (TPR): 37.79 average units and 91.47 average sales per record.

## Preliminary Business Recommendation

Evaluate a controlled test of the combined Feature + Display promotion. Measure incremental sales, promotional costs, and contribution margin before making a pricing decision.

## Limitations

The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causality or profitability. Promotional results may vary by store, week, price, and demand conditions. Product and promotional costs have not yet been incorporated.
